Optional 1 SOL wager · Devnet

Player 1 creates the match; Player 2 joins it. The site’s local Devnet sponsor wallet is the referee and automatically pays the winner after the game. Each player stakes 1 SOL; the winner receives 2 SOL. Players still need SOL for their deposits, and Player 1 also needs SOL for account rent and creation fees. Devnet testing only: the game result is reported by the browser and is not independently verified, so a dishonest player could request the wrong payout. Never use this automatic referee with real funds.

Switch the network selector to Devnet to enable wagers.
